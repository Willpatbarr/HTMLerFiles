Only two of these were open questions when the epic started. The router and the API framework were already settled in the E-LAM-0000 research documents — the tickets just had not been told.

Conventions now in force

  • Thin handlers. A handler parses a request and formats a response; anything that queries Postgres belongs in internal/document/ or a sibling.
  • Shared or feature. A component used by more than one feature, or with no domain knowledge, is shared; anything else stays in its feature.
  • Generated files are committed. api/openapi.json, schema.d.ts, and routeTree.gen.ts are all in git, and CI regenerates and diffs the first two.
  • Both CIs fail on drift. A handler change without a regeneration, or a spec pulled without regenerating types, is a red build rather than a runtime surprise.
  • New files carry OMLR comment boxes. Adopted in LAM-7; existing files are deliberately not backfilled.
