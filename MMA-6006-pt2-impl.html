The constraint the whole change is shaped around

No coordinator endpoint exists. The only write is PUT /v5/calendars/events/{eventId}, and DtoEventUpdate declares calendarId, name, description, startDate, endDate and timeZone as non-null.

explicitNulls = false cannot omit them, so every removal echoes the whole event back. The nullable fields (reminder, contact, audience) are omitted when null and survive untouched — which is what makes the echo safe even though EventDetails carries no reminder.
