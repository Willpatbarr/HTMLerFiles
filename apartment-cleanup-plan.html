Apartment Cleanup Plan

6 chores · about 1 hour 40 minutes · ordered so you never redo work

One-sentence version

Start the dishes so they soak while you clear every floor and surface, then vacuum last so nothing you just picked up ends up back on the ground.

The Order (and why)

Three rules decide the sequence. That's the whole strategy.

  • Wet work first. Dishes soak or run in the dishwasher while you do other things. Free time.
  • Floor-clearing before floor-cleaning. Legos and bedroom pickup must beat the vacuum.
  • Vacuum is always last. If you vacuum early, you'll do it twice.

The 6 Tasks

0 of 6 done

1

Wash dishes

Must 20 min

Do this first only to start it. Load and run, or fill the sink and walk away.

  • Load the dishwasher and start it, or fill the sink with hot soapy water
  • Leave the crusty pans to soak — come back at the end
  • Wipe the counter you just cleared while you're standing there
2

Put away legos

Must 15 min

Before the vacuum, not after. Vacuuming legos is a bad afternoon.

  • One container, one pass — do not sort by color or set
  • Sweep the floor with your hand under furniture edges
  • If a build is half-finished, move it somewhere off the floor intact
3

Clean bedroom

Must 20 min

This is the vague one, so make it concrete: everything on the floor goes into a pile, a bin, or the trash.

  • Trash bag first — one lap, throw out obvious garbage
  • Dirty clothes to the hamper, clean ones to the laundry pile for step 4
  • Make the bed — it makes the whole room read as "done"
  • Clear the nightstand and dresser top; floor completely clear when you finish
4

Fold laundry

Important 20 min

The sit-down task. Put a show on. It's the reward slot between two standing chores.

  • Dump it all on the now-made bed
  • Sort into 4 piles: shirts, pants, socks/underwear, hang-ups
  • Fold pile by pile, put each away before starting the next
  • Bed must be empty when you stop — no "finish it later" pile
5

Bathroom countertop

Important 10 min

Smallest task on the list. Fast win, big visual payoff.

  • Take everything off the counter
  • Toss empties and expired stuff — be ruthless, you won't use them
  • Wipe the counter and the sink basin
  • Put back only what you use daily; the rest goes in a drawer
6

Vacuum

Must · Last 15 min

Only start this when every floor is clear. That's the payoff for the order.

  • Bedroom, then living areas, then hallway — finish at the door
  • Don't move furniture; edges and open floor are enough
  • Empty the canister when you're done so future-you isn't blocked
Then: circle back and finish the soaking pans from step 1. That's the actual last thing.

Remember Only 3 Things

1Start the dishes first, finish them last.
2Legos and bedroom floor before the vacuum.
3Vacuum is the finish line. Nothing after it.

What To Do Next

  1. Right now: load and start the dishwasher, or fill the sink. Two minutes, and it works in the background.
  2. Grab a trash bag and a lego bin before you sit down again — having the container in hand is what makes steps 2 and 3 actually happen.
  3. Set a 20-minute timer per task. When it goes off, move on even if it's imperfect. The order matters more than any single task being perfect.
  4. Check the boxes above as you go — the bar at the top of the task list fills in.

Total: about 1 hour 40 minutes · 3 must-do, 2 important, all 6 fit in one afternoon
